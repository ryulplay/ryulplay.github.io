Slime Toss Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

Ryulplay (률플레이) ("the developer") makes and provides the mobile game "Slime Toss" ("the game"). This policy explains how the game handles your information.

1. Personal information we collect

The game does not collect personal information.

  • There is no account and no login.
  • The game does not connect to the internet and sends no information off your device.
  • There are no ads and no analytics tools.
  • The game does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

2. What is stored on your device

Your progress (cleared stages, slimes, settings) is saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. The developer cannot see this file.

3. Retention and deletion

The save file is removed when you uninstall the game or clear the game's data in your device settings. The developer keeps no information, so there is nothing to request deletion of.

4. Sharing, processing by others, transfer abroad

None. The developer does not give your information to third parties, does not hand it to processors, and does not transfer it to other countries.

5. Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

6. Your rights

You have the right to ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information. The game holds none, but you can contact us with any question.

7. Contact (privacy officer)

  • Name: Ryulplay (률플레이)
  • Email: the address under "Developer contact" on the Slime Toss page in Google Play

8. Changes

If the game adds features such as ads or purchases that change how information is handled, this page will be updated, with the date, before that version is released.
