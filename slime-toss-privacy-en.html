Slime Toss Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21

Ryulstudio (률스튜디오), publishing games under the brand Ryulplay ("the developer") makes and provides the mobile game "Slime Toss" ("the game"). This policy explains how the game handles your information.

1. Summary

  • The developer does not collect information that identifies you, such as your name or contact details. There is no account, no login and no developer server.
  • The game has rewarded ads that play only when you choose to tap for a reward. To show these ads, Google's advertising service (AdMob) receives some information from your device.
  • To improve the game, the developer receives usage statistics (where players stop, how long they play, which features they use) through Google Analytics for Firebase. You can turn this off at any time with "분석 데이터 보내기" (Send analytics data) in Settings.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.

The game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK from Google LLC. The SDK sends the following from your device to Google. The developer does not receive this information and only sees aggregated statistics in AdMob.

3. Sharing and international transfer

The information in section 2 is sent to Google LLC (AdMob) for ad serving, analytics and fraud prevention, over an encrypted connection, when the game loads or shows an ad. The information in section 2-2 is processed by Google LLC (Google Analytics for Firebase) on the developer's behalf for usage statistics, sent from time to time while you play over an encrypted connection, and its event data is kept for 2 months. Both may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. The developer does not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

4. How to opt out of personalised ads

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID: device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID". Menu names vary by device.
  • Turn off ad personalisation for your Google account: https://myadcenter.google.com
  • Do not watch ads: ads play only when you tap the button. You can finish the game without them.
  • In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent message appears on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time under "Privacy choices" in Settings.
  • Turn off usage statistics: switch off "분석 데이터 보내기" (Send analytics data) in Settings (section 2-2).

5. Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (the age set by Korean law). Regardless of age, the game requests only non-personalised ads for all players, marks every ad request as coming from a user under the age of consent, and accepts only ads rated for teens (T) or lower. Google states that it does not personalise ads or use remarketing for requests marked this way and does not use the advertising ID for them (https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/targeting). Parents or guardians with questions can use the contact in section 7.

6. Data on your device, retention and deletion

Your progress, the daily counters for ad buttons and your "Send analytics data" choice are stored only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. The developer holds no information that identifies you. Usage statistics are deleted after the period in section 2-2. You can manage the ad data Google holds through the options in section 4 and through "My Activity" in your Google account.
